Sign in without a password

A passkey uses whatever unlock your device already has —face, fingerprint, iris or PIN on a phone or laptop,Windows Hello or Touch ID / Face ID on a computer, or a security key for the ones that have no scanner at all. Fingerprint is never required: a phone with only a PIN works, and a passkey saved in Google Password Manager or iCloud Keychain signs you in on any device you own. Nothing secret ever leaves your device, and Google sign-in keeps working alongside it.

Sign in

The button asks your device for whatever it can do — face, fingerprint, PIN, or a phone you hold up to scan the QR. Pick the account first if you use an older security key.

On a computer with no scanner, the first button also offers“use a phone” — scan the QR with your phone and confirm with that phone's face, fingerprint or PIN. No passkey yet?Sign in with Googlefirst, then add one below.

Add a passkey

Signed in as . Add one per device you sign in from — the next sign-in is one tap, one look, or one PIN.

“Let the browser choose” is the safe default: it offers a passkey stored on this device, a synced passkey from Google Password Manager or iCloud Keychain, a phone over QR, or a security key — whichever the device supports. Nothing here requires a fingerprint.
