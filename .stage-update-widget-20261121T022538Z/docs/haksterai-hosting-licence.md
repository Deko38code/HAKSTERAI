# HaksterAI — Website hosting licence & service contract

**Version:** 2026-11-16 · **Revision:** 2 · **Effective:** 2026-11-16
**Provider:** HaksterAI — website builder and hosting provider (ABN shown on the invoice)
**Client:** the account holder named on the account invoice for this website
**Support and notices:** support@haksterai.com
**Currency:** all amounts are in United States dollars (USD) unless an invoice states otherwise

> This is the provider's record of the agreement. The client copy is supplied on request, by email or as
> a printed PDF from the provider's records. It is never published on the client's public website.

## 1 · Definitions

- **Build total** — the agreed price of the website build recorded on the account invoice. For the current
  Junkalot engagement the recorded build total is **$15,375.99 USD** (build $15,000.00 + portfolio
  maintenance $375.99).
- **Confirmed payment** — a payment Stripe has confirmed as paid. Pending, failed, reversed and
  charged-back payments are not confirmed payments.
- **Build balance** — the build total less every confirmed payment. Only confirmed payments reduce it, and
  no payment is counted twice.
- **Monthly payment** — **$249.00 USD** per month while the build balance is open, and the care plan fee
  under section 3.2 after the build is paid in full.
- **Due date** — 00:00 UTC (8:00 am Australian Western Standard Time) on the first day of each month.
- **Grace period** — the **7 days** after the due date, ending at 00:00 UTC on the eighth day of the month.
- **Overdue** — a payment not confirmed by the end of its grace period.
- **Outstanding balance** — the overdue monthly payments stated in a notice under section 5, excluding
  any late interest and sale costs. Paying it in full removes that state; a dispute about the amount is
  dealt with under section 4.9.
- **Lien sale state** — the provider's contractual right under section 5 to resell its own design and
  code. It is **not** a retention of title, a security interest, a charge or a mortgage, and the provider
  acquires no interest in any property of the client.
- **Admin area** — the dashboard where the client edits the site and views bookings and invoices.
- **Public site** — the website the client's visitors see.
- **Business day** — a day other than a Saturday, Sunday or public holiday in Western Australia.
- **Portfolio record** — the provider's per-client record described in section 8.8.

## 2 · Scope

The engagement covers the website build described on the invoice, hosting and server capacity for it,
booking and email functions, the AI visitor chat and coaching assistant attached to the site, security
monitoring, and routine care: software and security updates, backups, monitoring, deploys of reviewed
changes, and support by email. Anything outside that list — new pages, new features, new integrations,
content writing, migrations to another platform — is a separate, quoted engagement.

## 3 · Build balance and payoff

1. The build balance is repaid by the monthly payment. Each confirmed monthly payment reduces it by the
   full monthly amount while the balance is open; hosting, care and support for that month are included in
   the payment.
2. When the build balance reaches zero the build is paid in full. The care, hosting and support plan then
   continues month to month at **$249.00 USD**. Either party may end it on 30 days' written notice; if the
   client ends it, hosting and support stop at the end of the notice period and the build is delivered
   under section 8.9. A change to that rate is a fee change under section 12.
3. The projected payoff month is calculated from the current balance at the monthly payment and shown on
   the account's licence card and invoices page. It changes only when the recorded balance, payments or
   monthly payment change.
4. If the provider records an additional agreed charge, it is added to the build total with the client's
   written agreement and appears on the invoice record before it is billed.

## 4 · Monthly payment, grace period and admin lock

1. The monthly payment falls due on the due date, beginning when the Pay button activates. If the Pay
   button activates after the 20th of a month, the first monthly payment falls due on the first day of the
   following month. The activation date is recorded on the account and shown in the admin area.
2. A payment is in its **grace period** until 00:00 UTC on the eighth day of the month. Inside it the
   account and the admin area remain fully available and no restriction applies.
3. If the payment is still unconfirmed when the grace period ends, the provider may **lock the client's
   admin area**. A locked admin area still lets the client sign in, view and export its bookings, invoices,
   content and customer data; see the amount due, the date it became overdue, the date of any lien sale
   notice and any cure deadline, and how to dispute the amount; and pay. The lock disables **only** content
   editing, publishing and settings changes.
4. Access returns when Stripe confirms the payment, and in any event within one business day. If automatic
   release fails, the provider releases the lock on written request within one business day.
5. **The public site keeps serving throughout.** The lock restricts the client's admin access only. It does
   not stop the website, its hosting, its internet connection or any domain name, and it does not take the
   site offline or redirect it.
6. Before the lock takes effect, the provider emails the account email address at the due date and again
   when the grace period ends, stating the amount due, when the grace period ends and the consequence. If
   an automatic notice cannot be sent, the provider sends it manually and records the date; the lock does
   not take effect before that notice is sent.
7. The builder/owner account is exempt from the lock.
8. While the admin area is locked, the client may still request an export of its own content and customer
   data by email; section 11 applies.
9. **No lock, lien sale state, acceleration or late interest applies while**
   (a) Stripe's record shows the payment as processing;
   (b) the client has notified a dispute about the amount in writing and it is unresolved; or
   (c) the notice in subsection 6 has not been sent.
   Where the admin area cannot read the live ledger, it shows "record unavailable" and the last amount
   Stripe confirmed, clearly labelled, and neither a lock, the lien sale state nor acceleration follows
   from an unreadable figure.

## 5 · Lien sale state

1. If the payment is still unconfirmed **30 days after the grace period ends**, or if **3 consecutive
   monthly payments are unconfirmed**, the account enters the **lien sale state**. Where both triggers
   apply, the earlier governs.
2. The provider must send written notice of the lien sale state to the account email address on the day it
   is recorded, stating the amount claimed, the cure deadline and the consequence in subsection 5. The
   state begins on the date that notice is sent, and the admin area shows the state and the cure deadline.
3. A chargeback or reversal reopens the affected month from the date the provider notifies the client of
   it, and a fresh grace period under section 4 runs from that notification. No lock, lien sale state or
   acceleration applies before that grace period ends.
4. The client then has **30 days from the notice** (the **cure deadline**) to settle the outstanding
   balance. If the balance is settled by the cure deadline, the lien sale state is removed and the account
   returns to normal.
5. If the balance is not settled by the cure deadline, the provider may sell the finished build — its own
   design and code created for it — to recover the outstanding balance and reasonable sale costs. **The
   client's brand name, content, images, domain name and customer data are never sold or transferred by
   that sale without the client's written consent.**
6. Before any sale the provider must:
   (a) send written notice to the account email address at least **30 days before the intended sale date**,
   stating the amount claimed, the sale date and that the client may settle the amount before that date to
   stop the sale;
   (b) remove the client's brand, trade marks, content, images and customer data from the build offered for
   sale, and certify in writing that it has done so. The provider preserves every copy it holds in its
   records, never deletes it, and the client may request an export at any time;
   (c) offer the build for sale at a reasonable price for at least 30 days and use reasonable efforts to
   obtain a fair price;
   (d) give the client a written account of the sale price, the reasonable sale costs and any surplus
   within 14 days of settlement, and pay the surplus at the same time.
7. A completed sale ends the client's licence to use that build. The client keeps its brand, content,
   domain name and customer data, and receives an export of them within 10 business days.
8. If no sale is completed within **6 months** of the cure deadline, the right to sell lapses, and the
   balance remains a debt recoverable in the ordinary way.
9. Nothing in this section gives the provider any interest in the client's property, or authorises it to
   delete the client's data or take the public site offline before a sale is completed.

## 6 · Payments, receipts and records

- Payments are collected only through the provider's Stripe hosted checkout. The provider never asks for
  card numbers and never stores card data.
- A receipt is sent only after Stripe confirms the payment. A pending, failed or reversed payment is never
  described as paid.
- A chargeback or reversal is treated as a payment that was never made, subject to section 5.3.
- Overdue amounts carry interest at the lower of 1.5% per month or the maximum permitted by law, from the
  end of the grace period until paid, subject to section 4.9.
- The payment ledger, this contract and the account's lock history are kept in the provider's records on
  haksterai.com and in the account's admin area. They are **not** published on the client's public website.
- If a displayed figure disagrees with Stripe's record, Stripe's confirmed record governs and the provider
  corrects the ledger.

## 7 · Failed payments and acceleration

1. If a card is declined the provider may retry, and will email the account email address so another method
   can be used. A failed retry is not a payment.
2. If the build has not been sold by the cure deadline, the whole remaining build balance becomes
   immediately due on that deadline and the provider may recover it as a debt. Acceleration does not apply
   while Stripe shows a payment as processing, or while a dispute notified under section 4.9 is unresolved.

## 8 · Licence grant and intellectual property

1. The provider grants the client a non-exclusive licence to use the delivered website, its content and its
   configured integrations while the account is in good standing and payments under this agreement are
   being made.
2. The client's own brand, text, images and customer data remain the client's.
3. The provider's platform code, AI configuration, infrastructure, automation and internal tooling remain
   the provider's and are not transferred.
4. **On payment in full** the provider assigns the client its copyright in the custom design and code
   created for this build. Third-party, open-source and provider platform components remain under their own
   licences and are licensed to the client for use with this site.
5. Domain names, payment accounts and email services connected for the site remain under the provider's
   control until the build is paid in full and ownership is transferred in writing, unless the parties
   agree otherwise in writing.
6. **Platform ownership until paid.** The site at **junkalot.co** runs as part of the provider's platform —
   **HaksterAI (haksterai.com)** and **PhantomIDE (phantomide.com)** — and remains part of that platform,
   under the provider's name, hosting and control, until the build balance is **paid in full** or the lien
   sale state in section 5 applies. The client's licence does not transfer the domain, the hosting or the
   platform accounts before that point. Ownership of the domain and hosting transfers to the client on
   payment in full, or earlier if the parties agree in writing.
7. **Proprietary software, always.** The website, the platform and everything the provider supplied with it
   are proprietary software. The client receives a licence to use the delivered build for its own business.
   It never receives ownership of the platform, a source-code release, a right to resell, sublicense,
   redistribute, publish or offer the build as a product, template or hosted service to anyone else, or a
   right to reverse-engineer, decompile, strip attribution from or reuse the platform components to build a
   competing product. The platform code, AI configuration, infrastructure, automation, internal tooling and
   the provider's methods remain the provider's proprietary property at all times, before and after payment
   in full, and are licensed — not sold, not assigned and not open-sourced — to the client. Only the custom
   design and code for this build is assigned under subsection 4, and that assignment is limited to this
   site and carries this subsection with it.
8. **Portfolio record.** Every site or application the provider builds and delivers — whether sold as a
   plan, or built through **PhantomIDE** or **HaksterAI** — is entered in the provider's client portfolio
   at delivery, with a stable client ID, an audit ID and record IDs for its licence version, build and
   invoices. The record holds the live balance and lock state, is kept in the provider's records only, and
   is available to the client on request. Selling or transferring a build transfers its portfolio record
   to the new owner's name; it is never renumbered.
9. When the build is paid in full, the provider delivers within 30 days a deployable copy of the build
   (source, assets and configuration), a written list of the provider platform components it depends on,
   and the terms on which those components continue to be licensed. The client may host the build
   elsewhere.

## 9 · Service standard

- The provider maintains the server, deploys, HTTPS and the connected services with reasonable care, and
  monitors them.
- Availability is best-effort. Third-party providers, networks and the client's own DNS can fail outside
  the provider's control, and no uptime percentage is promised unless agreed separately in writing.
- Changes are reviewed and deployed by the provider. Automatic updates only ever apply reviewed builds.
- The provider makes reasonable efforts to keep backups and does not delete client work; a specific
  recovery point is not guaranteed.
- Defects in delivered work reported within 30 days of launch are fixed at no charge. **Launch** means
  the later of the date the public site is first served and the date the client accepts this agreement,
  so accepting an already-live site starts its own 30-day window. New requests are quoted under
  section 2.

## 10 · Client responsibilities and indemnity

- Keep the account email address current; receipts, notices and lock warnings are sent to it.
- Supply content, images and customer data the client has the right to use, and keep those rights current.
- Do not use the site for unlawful content or activity, and do not interfere with the server, other
  accounts or the platform's security.
- Do not share admin credentials; report suspected unauthorised access promptly.
- Each party indemnifies the other against third-party claims to the extent caused by that party's own
  breach of this agreement, negligence or unlawful conduct — the client for content, images and data it
  supplied, the provider for content and code it supplied. The indemnity does not extend to the other
  party's own negligence, wilful misconduct or breach, and is subject to section 15 except for fraud or
  wilful misconduct.

## 11 · Data, privacy and the AI assistant

- The client is the controller of its visitors' data. The provider processes it to run the site and the
  services described in section 2.
- The client may request an export of its content and customer data at any time; the provider supplies it
  within 10 business days.
- **The provider never deletes the client's data unless the client asks.** Its content, images, customer
  data, records and the site itself are not deleted at any time — not on non-payment, not when the admin
  area is locked, not in the lien sale state and not on termination. Data is deleted only when the client
  asks by email to support@haksterai.com (or another address the provider records for that account), and
  then only after the provider has confirmed the export and kept the records it must keep for tax, legal or
  dispute purposes. A sale under section 5 never deletes client data either.
- The provider notifies the client without undue delay after becoming aware of unauthorised access to, or
  disclosure of, the client's content or customer data, and takes reasonable steps to contain it and to
  assist the client's own notification obligations.
- AI assistant output can be inaccurate. The client reviews AI output before relying on it or publishing
  it to its own customers. AI usage is subject to fair use so that one account cannot degrade the service
  for others.

## 12 · Fees, changes and notices

- The monthly payment stays at **$249.00 USD** until a fee change takes effect under this section.
- A fee change needs **at least 30 days' written notice** to the account email address, applies only to
  future months, and the client may end the agreement before it takes effect without penalty.
- Notices are given to the account email address and are treated as received on the next business day. The
  provider's records are the record of what was sent and when.

## 13 · Term, termination and refunds

- Either party may end the agreement with 30 days' written notice. Amounts due for work already performed
  and months already served remain payable.
- On termination the public site continues to be served for 30 days after the notice period ends so the
  client can arrange a transfer, and the provider delivers the client's content and customer data within
  10 business days.
- If the provider ends the agreement for its own convenience, it refunds amounts received for work not yet
  performed and any unused whole months within 14 days.
- Build payments already applied to completed work are not refundable, except where a refund is required
  by law.
- The provider keeps copies of the site and its records after termination so the client's data is not
  lost. Retention is not a claim of ownership.

## 14 · Right to refuse service

1. The provider may **refuse a new order, build, migration or hosting request at intake for any lawful
   reason before payment is taken**.
2. For an existing engagement the provider may suspend or end service only for non-payment dealt with under
   section 4, unlawful content or activity, abusive conduct, a security compromise, or where continuing
   would breach a law or a third-party provider's terms. Except in an emergency, the provider gives 7 days'
   written notice and an opportunity to remedy.
3. If the provider declines or ends an engagement after taking payment and the client is not in default, it
   refunds amounts received for work not yet performed within 14 days and, where payments received exceed
   the value of the work performed, refunds the difference. The client keeps a licence to use the build as
   delivered at the date of refusal and receives its content and customer data within 10 business days.
4. A refusal is recorded in the provider's records with its date and reason.

## 15 · Liability

- To the extent permitted by law, the provider is not liable for indirect or consequential loss, lost
  profit, lost bookings, or loss caused by third-party services, networks or the client's own changes.
- The provider's total liability for any claim is limited to the greater of the amounts the client paid in
  the three months before the claim, or the amounts the client paid toward the build.
- Nothing excludes a liability that cannot be excluded by law, including consumer guarantees that apply
  to the client.
- The privacy obligations in section 11 are not limited by this section.

## 16 · Governing law and disputes

- This agreement is governed by the laws of Western Australia, and the courts of Western Australia have
  exclusive jurisdiction.
- The parties will attempt to resolve a dispute in good faith for 30 days before starting proceedings.
  Mediation is a precondition to proceedings.

## 17 · Acceptance and changes

1. The client accepts this version by signing it, or by written confirmation by email that quotes the
   version number and date. Continued use of the site, or a monthly payment, is not by itself acceptance
   of a change to sections 4, 5, 7, 8 or 14.
2. The provider may change fees, service scope and other non-material terms with at least 30 days' written
   notice to the account email address, applying only to future months. The client may end the agreement
   before a change takes effect without penalty. A change to sections 4, 5, 7, 8 or 14 takes effect only
   with the client's written agreement.
3. Each version is recorded with the account, the version number and a UTC timestamp.

## 18 · General

- **Entire agreement.** This document, its schedules and the invoice record accepted in writing by the
  client are the whole agreement. An amount in the invoice record governs only to the extent the client
  accepted that record in writing; otherwise this document governs.
- **GST and ABN.** Amounts are in USD and exclude GST unless an invoice states otherwise. Where GST
  applies, the invoice is a tax invoice showing the provider's ABN and the GST amount.
- **Assignment.** Neither party may assign this agreement without the other's written consent, except to
  a successor of the whole business.
- **Subcontractors.** The provider may use hosting, payment and email providers to deliver the service and
  remains responsible for the parts it controls.
- **Force majeure.** Neither party is liable for delay caused by events outside its reasonable control.
- **Survival.** Sections 5, 6, 8, 10, 11, 13, 15 and 16 survive termination.
- **Severability and waiver.** If a provision is unenforceable the rest continues to apply; a waiver is not
  effective unless in writing.
